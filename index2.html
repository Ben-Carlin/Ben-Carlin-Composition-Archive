The Story Behind The Sound

A Classical Ear Meets Storybooks

My musical journey started with learning Nintendo music on a keyboard, which lead me to study classical piano in my teens. Now I've gone full circle. Over the past 7+ years of composing, I've combined that foundation with my passion for stories and however they might be told: through films, games, or any form of art.

I prefer writing music that focuses on the small narrative details rather than trying to be huge and epic all the time. For whichever artform it may be, I just want the music to feel natural and make the emotions of the scene shine.

Melodic Mindfulness

The right melody can add a new axis of emotional story telling—I like finding it.

Harmonies in the Heart Strings

I aquired a rich pallet of harmonies through my experience in romantic-impressionistic music, and study of japanese soundtracks.
